A long tradition

Service has always been part of the 30th house

In November 1931, Alpha Theta and the Gamma Chapter of Kappa Alpha Psi Fraternity, Inc. renewed their annual charity football game before about five hundred spectators, raising a collective $250 for the unemployed. In March 1932, the brothers hosted Langston Hughes during his visit to the University of Iowa.
